# Kia ora, I'm Jove 🤘

For years I've run CNC laser and plasma cutters, turning drawings into steel parts where a millimetre out means scrap. Now I'm taking that same mindset into software.

I'm a Year 2 **Diploma in Software Development** student at Yoobee in Tauranga, New Zealand. I like building things for real people, including an accessible marketplace for older New Zealanders, an activity-matching tool for disability support, and the software side of my own 3D printing business.

## 🔧 What the shop floor taught me (and how I use it in code)

- **Read the drawing twice, cut once.** I write the requirements and plan before I write the code.
- **Measure, don't guess.** I test against the spec and check the logs before deciding something works.
- **Downtime costs money.** I care about things like monitoring, alarms and fixing the root cause, not just the symptom.
- **The job ships on time.** Years of shift work taught me to turn up, finish the job and hand it over properly.

## 🎯 What I'm looking for

Junior developer, cloud or IT support roles. I'm especially interested in teams building software for manufacturing, operations or underserved communities, where it helps to know how things work on the floor.
